# Theming and backgrounds

## Titanium design language

The launcher uses a deliberately physical interface:

- Deep black surfaces create separation and contrast.
- Brushed stainless-steel and titanium gradients simulate cylindrical reflection.
- Fine diamond crosshatching gives buttons and orbital nodes a knurled grip texture.
- Bright top specular bands and hard lower shadows make controls feel raised.
- Cyan anodizing communicates selection and active controls.
- Amber is reserved for radio status and circuit energy.

Controls lift slightly on hover and depress on click to reinforce the mechanical behavior.

## Preparing a background

For best results, use:

- 16:9 or screen-native aspect ratio
- At least 1920 × 1080 pixels
- A dark image with a clear central or lower-center focal point
- Limited fine detail behind text and the right-side panels
- Cyan and amber highlights that complement the interface

Supported chooser formats are JPG, JPEG, PNG, BMP, GIF, and WebP. WPF codec support can vary by Windows installation, especially for animated WebP.

## Applying a background

1. Open Titanium Orbital Launcher.
2. Select **Background**.
3. Choose the image.
4. Confirm that labels and radio controls remain readable.
5. Select **Default** at any time to restore the bundled circuit wallpaper.

The launcher applies a slow scale-and-drift movement to create a living-wallpaper effect while retaining a dark overlay for legibility.

## Suggested visual recipe

Use a dark macro photograph or rendered machine surface with:

- matte black or gunmetal foundation
- brushed titanium edges
- a small number of warm amber light points
- one or two cyan signal lights
- shallow depth of field
- no embedded text or logos

Avoid bright white backgrounds, dense text, faces beneath the orbit, or important detail at window edges where cropping can occur.
